Full-run comparison under identical demand

MetricOpen roadLane closureClosure + fixed metering
Compare open-road and closure cases first, then compare unmetered and fixed-metered ramp entry under the same closure. All three share arrivals and driver parameters. Fixed metering is a baseline, not an adaptive TMC.
Model scope: Three lanes, deterministic arrivals, desired speeds of 25–31 m/s, IDM following, and simplified MOBIL-inspired lane changes. Work-zone merges require acceptable front/rear gaps and braking. A simplified alternating-yield rule lets a following vehicle open a gap and then receive its turn. There is no continuous lateral dynamics or calibrated courtesy model. The lane-ending obstacle, rather than a speed zone, produces slowing. Delay uses each driver’s desired speed and includes boundary waits and unfinished trips. Charts use truth; detector CSV provides the observation interface. Default demand rises at 240 s and falls at 480 s. Timestep sensitivity is reported; these behavioral assumptions are not empirically calibrated.
IDM: Treiber, Hennecke & Helbing (2000), Physical Review E 62, 1805–1824. · MOBIL: Kesting, Treiber & Helbing (2007). · Teaching prototype; not empirically calibrated.
