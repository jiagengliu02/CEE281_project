CEE281 / TRAFFIC SIMULATION PROJECT

Explore two traffic environments

Open a saved simulation, inspect the flow, and compare control decisions. Both viewers work offline and contain their own data.

01 / FREEWAY + RAMP

Following, merging, and ramp entry

Watch vehicles move along a three-lane freeway with an entry ramp. Compare an open road, a work-zone closure, and fixed ramp metering. Inspect trajectories, queues, detector flow, and speed.

Open freeway and ramp

For a ramp-control comparison, select the two closure cases. The moving road view represents simulated vehicle positions.

02 / SIGNALIZED INTERSECTION

Phases, queues, and adaptive timing

Compare fixed-time and queue-responsive signals under the same arrivals. Explore a demand shift, detector faults, and overload; inspect signal timing, estimated queues, and delay.

Open signalized intersection

Blocks represent queue counts, not spatial vehicle trajectories. The instructor-supplied scaffold has not yet been integrated.
